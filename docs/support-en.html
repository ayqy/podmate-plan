MeowPodcast Support

MeowPodcast (project name: Podmate) helps you create interviews and article podcasts, and practise speaking. App availability is determined by its App Store listing.

Language and on-device narration

Choose System, English or 简体中文 in Settings. Your explicit choice is saved. On-device article narration currently uses Chinese voices; changing the interface language does not translate your original text. Finished podcasts can be shared as MP3 or WAV.

Contact support

For product or privacy questions, email nwujiajie@163.com. Do not send passwords, API keys or private recordings.

You can also use the public issue tracker to contact maintainer ayqy. Include the app version, device, OS version, steps and a description without personal information. Public issues are visible to everyone; remove personal information from screenshots.

Privacy and originals

Projects are stored on your device by default. Optional uploads and remote copies are explained in the privacy policy. Keep your only originals and backups when troubleshooting.

Third-party licenses and source

MP3 encoding uses LAME. Download the license notices, integrated LAME source and version-matched relinking materials. These materials include library source, licenses, application objects, resources and build instructions.
